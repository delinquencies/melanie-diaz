Story circles → vertical viewer

Built from the Hershesons / Storista measurements in docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md §2.5. Story 1 uses the existing Final.mp4. The other stories are placeholders (Melanie's existing photos shown as timed image stories) until real 9:16 clips are available. Ring colours are temporary tokens, not a palette decision.

Stories

Try

  • Hover a circle (mouse) to preview the clip inside the ring.
  • Open a story. Navigate with ▲ ▼, arrow keys, mouse wheel, or a vertical swipe on a phone.
  • Tap the video to pause or play. Use the speaker button or the "Tap to unmute" pill for sound.
  • Close with ✕, Esc, or the browser Back button. Focus returns to the circle you opened.
  • Stories auto-advance when they finish. The last one closes the viewer.
